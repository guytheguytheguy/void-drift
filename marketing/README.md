## What Is VoidDrift?

VoidDrift is a free-to-play browser-based 3D infinite runner where the core mechanic is not speed alone — it is *dimension shifting*. At any moment the player inhabits one of three parallel dimensions: Crimson, Cyan, or Violet. Each dimension carries its own obstacle geometry, visual palette, and hazard timing. Surviving means reading the void across all three planes simultaneously and flipping dimensions at exactly the right instant.

No download. No install. Open a browser tab and you are in the void.

---

## The Dimension-Shifting Mechanic

Most runners ask one question: how long can you react? VoidDrift asks a second question simultaneously — *which reality do you want to react in?*

- **Crimson Dimension** — dense obstacle fields rendered in deep red geometry; aggressive, punishing pacing
- **Cyan Dimension** — gap-heavy terrain requiring precise timing through narrow corridors
- **Violet Dimension** — layered hazards that demand multiple rapid dimension transitions in sequence

Obstacles that block your path in Crimson are open channels in Cyan. Hazards filling Violet may not exist at all in Crimson. The skill ceiling emerges from learning when to shift — not just when to dodge. Players develop spatial memory across three overlapping track geometries, turning every run into a different puzzle.

## Global Leaderboard

Every run ends with a score. The top scores are permanently recorded in a global leaderboard powered by Supabase. Players compete across sessions, time zones, and devices. The leaderboard is the social hook — beating a score that has stood for days is its own reward.
