## Brand Position

VoidDrift is a **free-to-play, browser-native 3D infinite runner** with a unique dimension-phasing mechanic. Players navigate through three distinct dimensional planes (crimson, cyan, violet), each with their own obstacle patterns and visual identity, competing for a persistent global leaderboard. Built on Three.js and React Three Fiber — technology rarely deployed in browser games — VoidDrift delivers console-quality 3D at zero install cost.

### Positioning Statement
