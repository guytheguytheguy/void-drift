**Demographics**
- Age: 22–35 | Knowledge worker, student, or creative professional
- Gaming habits: Plays 15–30 min sessions during breaks; browser games as a quick escape
- Devices: Desktop/laptop primary; mobile secondary

**Goals**
- Quick, low-friction entertainment during work breaks or downtime

- Fresh gameplay that doesn't feel like another clone

**Pain Points**
- Most browser games are visually dated 2D clones
- Doesn't want to install another app for a 20-minute gaming session
- Hates energy meters, pop-up ads, and paywalls

**How VoidDrift Serves Jordan**
- Open in 3 seconds, beautiful 3D immediately
- No install, no ads, no friction
- Share-your-score button (planned) lets Jordan send their leaderboard rank to friends

**Goals**

**Pain Points**

**How VoidDrift Serves Alex**
- Real-time global Supabase leaderboard with persistent scores
- Pure skill mechanic — dimension phasing requires pattern recognition and reflex
- No pay-to-win; top scores are entirely skill-based
