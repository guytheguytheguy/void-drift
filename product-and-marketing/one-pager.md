# Void Drift — One-Pager
**Date:** 2026-03-14

## Elevator Pitch
Void Drift is a free-to-play browser-based 3D arcade game. Players phase through dimensions and drift through the void, competing for leaderboard positions with a global community. No install required.

## Target Market
- Casual gamers who want a quick browser-based experience
- Competitive players motivated by leaderboards
- Indie game enthusiasts looking for novel browser-native 3D experiences

## Key Differentiation
- **Browser-native 3D:** No download or plugin required — plays directly in the browser
- **Dimension mechanics:** Unique "phase through dimensions" gameplay concept with distinct dimension color systems
- **Free to play:** Zero barrier to entry; leaderboard drives engagement
- **Supabase-powered leaderboard:** Persistent global scoring with real-time updates
